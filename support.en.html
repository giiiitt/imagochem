Frequently asked questions

The window is empty, or only the Dock icon appears?

First check that your OS version meets the table above, then quit the app completely and open it again. If the problem persists, please email us a screenshot.

Does the app need a network connection or an account?

Neither. It works fully offline after installation, with no accounts, ads, or analytics. See the privacy policy for details.

Where are my saved lesson slides and structure files?

In the location you chose when saving, plus the app's local data directory. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Moving to another device means copying the files yourself.

What is the iPad version for?

The iPad version is designed as a classroom control surface: the teacher drives the 3D models and animations on the iPad, mirrors the picture to the classroom display, and can annotate directly on the visuals with Apple Pencil. Landscape orientation is recommended.

Do I have to buy again on a new Mac or iPad?

Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account — simply download from the App Store on the new device with the same account. The iPad and Mac editions are separate apps and are purchased separately.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please submit a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are there discounts for schools or institutions?

Yes. Schools can buy in bulk for teachers through Apple School Manager / Apple's education volume purchase programme, or email us at the address above.
