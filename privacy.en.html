ImagoChem Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · 中文

In one sentence: ImagoChem collects and transmits no personal information, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Everything you do in the app stays on your own device.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage history. The app has no account system and ships with no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking components.

2. Where your data lives

  • Your preferences (such as annotation font size, interface language, display options) are stored locally on your device.
  • Lesson plans, slides, snapshots, annotations, and the structure files you import or generate are stored locally, or in a location you choose.
  • All of it stays on your device. We cannot access it, and no server-side copy exists.

3. Network access

The app's core features — 3D molecular and crystal visualization, demonstration animations, structure editing and generation, lesson planning and slides — run entirely on your device and require no network connection. The app sends no data to us or to any third party while it runs. If you have enabled iCloud or another system-level backup, that backup content is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and is unrelated to us.

4. Files and system permissions

The app accesses only the file or folder you explicitly choose when you open, import, export, or save; it does not scan your disk or read files you did not select. The macOS version runs inside the App Sandbox.

5. Purchases and payments

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store and payment information is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment details; we only see the aggregated, non-identifying sales data Apple provides.

6. Children's privacy

ImagoChem is a classroom tool for teachers. Because we collect no information from anyone, we necessarily collect none from children.

7. Third-party components

The app bundles open-source components (such as Mol* and three.js). They run locally as part of the app, do not access the network, and collect no data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

9. Contact

For any question about this policy, contact: jccqsd@126.com
